SmileRoll Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

In short

SmileRoll doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't sell your data. If what we collect ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Your photos

With your permission, SmileRoll reads your photo library to make games. Choosing photos, recognizing scenes and making puzzles all happen on your iPhone. SmileRoll does not upload, sell or share your photos with us or anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

Spot the difference keeps a copy of the current puzzle (the photo and its changed version) inside the app. SmileRoll also keeps your stars, streak, settings, a list of photos already used (so they don't repeat) and place names it has looked up. All of this stays on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

Place names

To show where a photo was taken, SmileRoll sends the photo's location coordinates (never the photo) to Apple's map service, which returns a place name. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request. Pin it shows an Apple map, which loads from Apple like any map app, and Then & Now asks Apple Maps for the names of places near a photo's location (again, coordinates only, never the photo).

Camera (Move & find)

Move & find uses the camera to show your photos around you in augmented reality. The camera view is processed live on your iPhone to place them; it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Memory Films

Memory Films are made on your iPhone from your own photos and kept inside the app. They're only shared or saved to your library when you choose to.

Jigsaw

While a jigsaw is in progress, a copy of its photo is kept inside the app so you can continue later. It's deleted when you finish or start a new puzzle.

Learning what you enjoy

SmileRoll learns from how you play (which changes you find, which answers you get right, which photos you love) to make better games. This stays on your iPhone. Where Apple Intelligence is available, film titles are written by Apple's on-device model; nothing is sent to us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.

Statistics

Apple may share anonymous download numbers and crash reports with the developer if you've allowed sharing with app developers in iOS Settings. SmileRoll itself sends nothing.

Children

SmileRoll doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
