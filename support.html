Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up or login. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Games are made on your iPhone and your photos never leave it. See the privacy policy.

Why does it ask for all my photos?
Games are made from your whole library, so with only a few photos allowed you'll see the same ones again and some games may not work. You can change this anytime in Settings → Apps → SmileRoll → Photos.

How do I unlock more games?
Play any game each day to build your streak. Each new game unlocks at a streak milestone, or sooner with stars. You can see them all on the Progress tab.

Move & find doesn't place photos in my room.
Use good light, point at floors, tables and walls with some detail, and move your phone slowly. Or choose Sit, which works anywhere.

I lost my streak or stars after deleting the app.
Progress is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the app removes it.
